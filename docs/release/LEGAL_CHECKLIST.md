# Legal and Distribution Checklist (not legal advice)

- [ ] Clear STXIC Halo product name and proposed logo for trademarks and Store confusion.
- [ ] Confirm publisher identity, address/contact obligations and tax profile in Partner Center.
- [ ] Ensure original asset ownership, fonts license and README copyright notices.
- [ ] Review licenses and notices of all direct/transitive packages; check Rust dependencies and installer binaries.
- [ ] Confirm no source copied from GPL-v3 competitor projects without a compatible licensing decision.
- [ ] Adopt a realistic privacy policy covering hook data, paths, retention, analytics and eventual provider adapters.
- [ ] Ensure opt-in permissions and cancellation logic do not falsely approve AI actions.
- [ ] Complete Win32 installer signing, hosting, update policy and Store certification.
- [ ] Document anti-cheat and elevated-access risks before future FPS integrations.
- [ ] Add end-user terms/license and warranty disclosures tailored to actual publisher and jurisdiction.
- [ ] Validate use of provider names (Claude Code, Codex) as descriptive compatibility claims without implying endorsement.
